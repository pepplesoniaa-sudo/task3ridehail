# About this document
@SRC Built from docs/05_traceability.md (its first paragraph), docs/10_brief_checklist.md (section 4, the summary) and docs/09_open_items.md (section 6, owner decisions recorded).
@P This document describes what was built: a ride hailing database of 16 tables with 116 constraints, 42 indexes, 27 triggers and 18 functions, CONFIRMED in evidence/catalogue_check_016.txt, together with the API contracts (27 endpoints EP01 to EP27 and 3 internal jobs JB1 to JB3, in docs/06_api_contracts.md). No API was built, so nothing was run over HTTP. The brief checklist summary records requirements 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 and 8 as DONE, requirement 4 as PARTIAL (D5, D13 and D16 have written design assumptions but are still open owner decisions) and requirement 5 as PARTIAL (four class notes unfilled, and the GraphQL part not run). What closes the gaps is that the owner supplies six required images (three diagrams and three screenshots of the rejected inserts) and two optional plan screenshots, fills four class notes, and confirms or replaces the written assumptions for D5, D13 and D16.
@P To follow the document, read the labels first. CONFIRMED means the output was run and kept in evidence/; INFERRED means the claim was reasoned, not run. The requirement IDs let a reader follow a rule from requirement to table to test: U1 to U4 are the four users, A1 to A5 the five key actions, B0 to B10 the rules, and Q1 to Q6 the questions, and docs/05_traceability.md maps every requirement ID against what serves it.
