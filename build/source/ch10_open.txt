## Known limits
@P What the design does not do, or has not proved.
@B 62 bulk trips (299 history rows) have a history row numbered out of order (05 item 13, CONFIRMED in evidence/migration_010_seq_investigation.txt). Accepted by the owner. A repair would need the append-only trigger switched off, which nobody approved.
@B pg_hba trusts loopback and the local socket inside the container. Any process in the container connects as any role with no password. Only other addresses use scram-sha-256 (CONFIRMED in the login-role test). Not changed (05 item 20).
@B Only three table names were attacked in the search-path proofs (audit_log, trip_status_history, status_transition_rule). The other tables rely on the same two protections (migrations 013 and 014) but were not each attacked (05 item 21).
@B Almost no concurrency tests exist beyond two: a second insert of the same idempotency key waits and times out (55P03), and a second move of the same trip waits for the first. Nothing was raced for two drivers accepting the same trip, two refunds on one payment, or a repeated provider event (05 item 10).
@B No driver is installed, so the SQLSTATE field name is unconfirmed in any driver. 06 section 1.6 gives the names I expect for node-postgres, pgx, psycopg and JDBC from memory. Nothing was installed.
@B Nothing was run over HTTP: no API exists (the api\ folder is empty), no endpoint, no stream, no GraphQL, no webhook. The contracts were checked only as the SQL behind them (06, 07, 08).
@B The actor is claimed by the application. The database checks that the claimed actor may make a move; it cannot prove who the actor is. A login role that is a member of ridehail_app can claim support (CONFIRMED, 05 items 7 and 18). A superuser can still switch triggers off.
@B Q5 and Q6 are open. Q5, location retention, has no period chosen and is blocked on legal advice (NDPA 2023 was not read). Q6, a driver who never arrives, still lets the rider pay the fee if they cancel after assignment. Both are open owner decisions (D8 and D9).
