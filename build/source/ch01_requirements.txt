# Chapter 1. Requirements
@SRC docs/01_requirements.md
@P Riders request trips and see a fare estimate. Drivers accept and drive them; riders pay through an outside provider. Both sides rate each other; support can investigate any trip.

## 1. Users
@B U1 Rider.
@B U2 Driver.
@B U3 Support admin: reads trips and payments, cancels, refunds.
@B U4 Payment provider: external system; may report results twice or late.

## 2. Five key actions
@P Proposal kept unchanged: the five cover the money path. Cancellation (B9) is a state change, not a sixth action; the state machine handles it (B5).
@B A1 Rider requests a trip (pickup, dropoff, estimate). Success: one "requested" trip with a stored estimate. Fails: missing locations, outside service area, rider already active (B1), retry (B6).
@B A2 Driver accepts. Success: exactly one driver assigned. Fails: two accept at once, driver already active (B2), rider cancelled first.
@B A3 Driver moves trip to arrived, started, completed; rider watches. Success: steps recorded in order. Fails: steps skipped or repeated, location updates lost.
@B A4 Rider pays. One payment covers exactly one fare or one cancellation fee. Fails: declined card, timeout, duplicate callback. A retried payment request creates no second charge (B6).
@B A5 Both rate each other. Success: one rating per person per trip. Fails: trip not completed, window passed, rated twice (B7).

## 3. Rules that must always hold
@P Trip statuses: requested, assigned, arrived, started, completed, cancelled. Completed and cancelled are terminal. Refunds are rows on the payment side. A dispute is only a support note in the audit log.
@B B0 A trip is ACTIVE in the statuses requested, assigned, arrived and started.
@B B1 A rider has at most one active trip. Enforced by a database unique index.
@B B2 A driver has at most one active trip. Same enforcement. A trip with no driver yet counts for the rider only.
@B B3 Money is whole kobo in integer columns, currency NGN only. Enforced by column types and a currency check. A decimal amount is stopped by API validation, not by the column type (the database rounds 10.5 kobo to 11; see docs\04 question 2).
@B B4 After completion, fare, distance, start and end times, rider and driver are frozen. Only updated_at may change. Enforced by a database trigger. Corrections and partial refunds are new rows.
@B B5 Every status change is recorded (who, when, from, to); only legal changes are allowed.
@B B6 Idempotency keys are unique per rider and action, not global. A retried request must not create a second trip or a second charge; it returns the stored result unchanged. Provider payment references are unique.
@B B7 A rating needs a completed trip and a participant, within 7 days of completion, once per person per trip.
@B B8 Personal data (names, phone numbers, locations) follows Nigeria's NDPA 2023: collect only what is needed, allow deletion, restrict access. Retention periods are NOT verified and need legal advice.
@B B9 Rider, driver or support may cancel from requested, assigned or arrived. The system cancels a requested trip no one accepted after request_timeout_seconds, with no fee (placeholder 600 seconds, needs owner approval, held in a settings row). After started, only support. A flat fee applies after assignment, none before. The fee is 50000 kobo (500 naira), a placeholder approved by the owner and held in a settings row, not hard coded.
@B B10 The sum of refunds on a payment never exceeds the payment amount.

## 4. Non goals
@B Matching algorithm, a live map or route display, surge pricing, promo codes, driver payouts, tax, multi-city and mobile apps are out of scope.
@B The rider sees the driver's latest position through the event stream (EP27), but no map or route is drawn.
@B A full dispute system is out of scope, and a money record for a manual refund of a failed payment is a known gap: its trace is provider_event plus audit_log.
